# Dr. Lamya Allıto

I am a PhD candidate in Biotechnology (currently in my second year) at Kafkas University, with a multidisciplinary background spanning Biotechnology and Food Engineering. My research focus lies at the intersection of biotechnology, functional food development, and computational data analysis, with a particular interest in therapeutic, resilient food technologies and scientific instrumentation.

## Academic Background & Research Interests

*   **PhD in Biotechnology (In Progress)** – Kafkas University
*   **M.Sc. in Biotechnology** – Kafkas University (Graduated 1st of Class with High Honors)
*   **B.Sc. in Food Engineering** – Kafkas University (Graduated 2nd of Class with High Honors)

My academic journey has driven me to explore the practical applications of molecular biology in food systems. My current research involves:
*   Characterizing bio-active compounds and functional food formulations.
*   Conducting molecular assays and analyzing cell-line studies.
*   Developing mathematical and kinetic models for microbial growth and shelf-life prediction.
*   Automating statistical analyses (such as $IC_{50}$ calculations) utilizing R and Python.
